Home Fusion

Browse your library by genre, rating and release date from the home screen.

Genres row
One card per genre, such as Action, Comedy or Romance.
Shuffles the genre cards each time Home loads. Otherwise genres are sorted by number of titles.

Hidden genres
These are the genres currently found in your library. Checked genres will not get a card in the Genres row.

Ratings row
Top 50, 8 - 10 Stars, 7, 6, 5, 4 Stars and 1 - 3 Stars.

Release Date row
Top 50 Latest, then each decade found in your library.

Content
Which library items the rows are built from.
Select at least one: movies or series.
Positions apply to all users. If a user's Home does not show the selected section, the row appears at the bottom instead. Rows sharing a position keep the order Genres, Ratings, Release Date.

The rows are injected automatically into the Jellyfin Web home screen. Native clients are not modified.
